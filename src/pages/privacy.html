Privacy Policy

This page explains how Zen Laundry uses the information you share with us through this website.

This is a plain-language starting draft. Zen Laundry should review it, add the effective date, and confirm it meets the data-protection requirements that apply in its location before launch.

Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us when you use our forms or contact us. Depending on the form, this may include:

  • Your name, or your organisation's name and contact person
  • Your phone number and/or email address
  • Your pickup address, if you request a pickup
  • The services you're interested in, your preferred dates and times, and any instructions or messages you send

How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry, laundry request or quote request
  • Contact you to confirm arrangements such as pickup, drop-off or delivery
  • Prepare a quotation for corporate enquiries

We do not sell your personal information.

How your information is handled

Form submissions are delivered to Zen Laundry through a form-processing service. If you contact us through WhatsApp, phone or email, your message is handled through that service.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information you've sent us at any time by contacting us.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please get in touch through our Contact page.
