About us

Laundry Made Simpler. That's Zen.

Zen Laundry is a laundry and garment-care business serving individual customers and organisations. We exist to take one more task off your list, so you can enjoy fresh, clean clothes with less stress.

Our Story

Laundry is one of those jobs that never really ends. Zen Laundry was created to make it easier — offering a dependable place to bring your clothes, and the convenience of pickup and delivery for those who need it.

Whether it's everyday washing and ironing, a special garment that needs extra care, or the laundry needs of a whole organisation, our aim is the same: clean, fresh results and a simple, friendly experience.
