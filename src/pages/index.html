    Fill in our Book a Pickup form with your details, the services you need and your pickup address. Your request goes to Zen Laundry, and we'll contact you to confirm availability and arrangements. A request is not a confirmed booking until we've confirmed it with you.

    Prefer messaging? You can also send us a WhatsApp message.

    Can I drop off my laundry in person?

    Yes. You're welcome to visit us and drop off your items in person.

    Which laundry services do you offer?

    We offer washing and ironing, pickup and delivery, dry cleaning, corporate laundry, duvet and bedsheet cleaning, and shoe and bag cleaning. See our Services page for details.

    Do you offer pickup and delivery in my area?

    Pickup and delivery availability depends on your location.

    Prices depend on the items and the service required. Get in touch or send a laundry request and we'll provide a quote.

    Processing time depends on the service and the items involved. Ask us when you make your request and we'll give you an estimate.

    Yes, we offer corporate laundry solutions for businesses and organisations. Tell us about your requirements through our corporate quote form and we'll get in touch to discuss them.
